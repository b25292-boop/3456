Simplified ideal AC generator. Coil area is fixed at 0.01 m². Internal resistance and mechanical losses are neglected.

Experiment Explanation

When water strikes the turbine, it rotates the shaft. The shaft rotates a magnet inside a stationary copper coil. This changes the magnetic flux through the coil and induces an alternating voltage according to Faraday's law. The induced current powers the bulb and produces alternating deflection in the galvanometer.
